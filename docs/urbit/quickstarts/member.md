# Work and Docs: member quickstart

Your operator supplies two addresses: your team's Stead home and the Stead
identity page on your individual ship. Use your own identity. Ask the operator
to grant access to a project if none appears after sign-in.

1. Open the home. Enter your identity ship and choose **Request sign-in**.
   Open your personal identity page separately and sign in to your ship there.
   Compare the home address and comparison code on both pages. Approve only
   when both match, then return to the home and choose **Check approval**.
2. Choose a project, then **Work**. If you have contributor access, choose
   **New work item**, enter a title and description, and **Save at home**.
   Wait for the saved confirmation. Another authorized member receives the
   change through live updates. Readers can view work but cannot save changes.
3. Open **Docs**, then **New collection**. Choose **Private drafts** for your
   personal drafts. A project maintainer can also create a shared collection.
   Select your collection, choose **New page**, and edit the body below the
   existing front matter. **Save page** stores canonical Markdown at the home.
4. To publish, save your draft first, then choose **Publish selected page** and
   a shared destination. Review the selected page and choose **Save at home**.
   Publishing copies that page; the source and other private pages stay private.
5. Use **Search** for authorized resources. **Links** connects a document and
   work item. **Activity** and **Inbox** show information visible to you.

If someone saves a newer revision, keep your draft open. Choose **Compare
current saved revision**, review the difference, and choose **Keep my edits
using this revision** only when you intend to save your text against that version.
Then save explicitly. Stead does not silently overwrite the other person's edit.

An uncertain save is not a saved confirmation. Keep the tab open. **Check receipt**
asks the home about the original request; **Retry this request** reuses that same
request. Reconnecting does not automatically submit another mutation. For a new
project, use **Retry this request**; receipt lookup requires
access to an existing project. A new collection remains unconfirmed if its
receipt is unavailable but your current project permissions still allow creation.
If another tab refreshed your session, **Resume this tab** reconnects it before you choose
what to retry. Browser drafts are held in memory: reloading or closing the tab
can lose unsaved text. Offline editing is not durable offline storage.

Choose **Sign out** when finished. Session loss and revoked access clear protected
views. **Support details** lets you review a small diagnostic report before
downloading it. You decide whether to share that file; Stead sends nothing
automatically. It excludes credentials, names, messages and document contents.

All controls can be reached with Tab and activated with Enter or Space. The
**Skip to project content** link skips repeated navigation. This alpha supports the
tested desktop Firefox profile; other browsers, assistive technologies and
mobile devices need their own recorded validation. Code, pull requests and
builds are not part of the general Work/Docs project interface.
