# Why Stead?

Stead gives a small team one place to answer three questions. Who’s doing the work? Why did we decide to do it? What actually changed? Your team runs that workspace on its Urbit ship. The writing stays in Markdown, and the code stays in Git.

Say a customer finds a bug in the setup instructions. The developer fixing the code and the person rewriting the guide can work from the same task. The next person answering that customer can open the task, read the decision and follow the link to the corrected guide.

## When would I choose it?

- **GitHub or GitLab.** If the pull request already tells the whole story, you’re in good shape. Stead earns its place when support, documentation or operations share responsibility for the outcome. A project can start with a task and a page and add a repository when it needs one.
- **Atlassian’s Jira and Confluence.** Both connect issues and pages. Choose Stead when you want one workspace your team runs, where your ship controls membership and you can take the project’s versioned writing and Git history with you.
- **A local folder.** It works well when the whole project is yours. Stead helps when you hand work to someone else. They can find the owner, the decision and the current page without asking you to piece the story together.
